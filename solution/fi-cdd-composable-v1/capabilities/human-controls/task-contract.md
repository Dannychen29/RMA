# Human control tasks

Create one structured task per unresolved control. Do not convert the task into an automated conclusion.

- Screening: Factimize or an approved alternative, candidate disposition and named reviewer evidence. GAP-005, GAP-006 and GAP-008 remain open.
- Geographic risk: approved effective version and institution/parent lookup evidence. GAP-007 remains open.
- Risk rating and Vostro override: preserve raw value, final value, reason and reviewer. GAP-010 and GAP-011 remain open.
- Prohibition and EDD routing: block negative or acceptance conclusions while a required input is missing.
- Recommendation and approval: require named Maker, Checker and Approver evidence. GAP-012 remains open.
- Final delivery: do not encrypt or submit until GAP-014 and GAP-015 are closed.

Resume at CAP-03 after new evidence is registered, then rerun CAP-04 and CAP-05.
